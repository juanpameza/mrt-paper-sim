Entry checklist

Every box, every time. No setup, no trade.

  1. Liquidity. Open interest at or above 1,000 on the strikes you'll use, bid/ask within a few cents on the spread.
  2. Volatility is live. Bollinger Bands sit outside the Keltner Channels. Bands inside the channels is a squeeze: skip it.
  3. Price at an extreme. Closing at or through the lower band for a bull call, the upper band for a bear put, and the move is out of character for the name.
  4. Momentum agrees. RSI at or below 30 for longs, at or above 70 for shorts. ADX under 40: a runaway trend is not a reversal candidate.
  5. No earnings inside the window. If the report lands before your planned exit, trade an index or ETF instead.
  6. Structure. $5-wide debit spread bought near half the width, so a full winner doubles. $10 width on names priced above about $400.
  7. Time. The monthly expiration 20 to 55 days out, closest to 35. Weeklies are thin and turn this into a coin flip; the sim only uses one when no monthly fits.
  8. Size. Total deployed stays under the cap. The rest is cash for corrections.
  9. Exits are set before entry. Close at a 50% loss or a 100% gain, whichever comes first, or at expiration, or if an earnings date moves inside the window. No averaging down.

The automated sim applies this list in order from the RULES block at the top of mrt_sim.py. Change a number there and the next run uses it; trades already open keep the exits they were opened with.
